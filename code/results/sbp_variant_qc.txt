READ:
- 'allele_mismatch' and 'non-SNV' are variants where the SBP record at that coordinate is a
  DIFFERENT variant from the instrument. Position-only matching cannot detect these; they are
  the exposure the reviewer identified.
- 'palindromic' variants are strand-ambiguous. harmonise_data(action=2) drops those with
  intermediate frequency downstream, so they are a bound rather than an error.
- The strict files keep only 'match' and 'strand_flip'. 63_sbp_strict_mr.R re-estimates
  every X->SBP edge on them, CAD->SBP above all.
