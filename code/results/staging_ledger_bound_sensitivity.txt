=== Bound sensitivity of the native-scale staging ledger ===

Negligibility bounds are clinical judgements, so every verdict is reported across a sweep of them.
Scenario bounds (binary log-odds / SBP mmHg / other per-SD):
  very tight  OR 1.01 / 0.5 mmHg / 0.02 SD
  tight       OR 1.02 / 0.5 mmHg / 0.02 SD
  PRIMARY     OR 1.05 / 1 mmHg / 0.05 SD
  loose       OR 1.10 / 2 mmHg / 0.10 SD
  very loose  OR 1.20 / 5 mmHg / 0.20 SD

THE LOAD-BEARING CLAIM IS BOUND-INVARIANT. The ledger's falsification claim is not the concordant
count but the direction of every reverse effect that is real. Under every scenario above, each
transition in the discordant family has a reverse edge running from a stage-4 disease into a
stage-2 trait — feedback or an index-event effect — and none reverses the staging order:
  very tight  n=4  SBP->CAD (reverse CAD->SBP, stage 4->2); T2D->CAD (reverse CAD->T2D, stage 4->2); T2D->HF (reverse HF->T2D, stage 4->2); T2D->Stroke (reverse Stroke->T2D, stage 4->2)
  tight       n=4  SBP->CAD (reverse CAD->SBP, stage 4->2); T2D->CAD (reverse CAD->T2D, stage 4->2); T2D->HF (reverse HF->T2D, stage 4->2); T2D->Stroke (reverse Stroke->T2D, stage 4->2)
  PRIMARY     n=4  SBP->CAD (reverse CAD->SBP, stage 4->2); T2D->CAD (reverse CAD->T2D, stage 4->2); T2D->HF (reverse HF->T2D, stage 4->2); T2D->Stroke (reverse Stroke->T2D, stage 4->2)
  loose       n=3  T2D->CAD (reverse CAD->T2D, stage 4->2); T2D->HF (reverse HF->T2D, stage 4->2); T2D->Stroke (reverse Stroke->T2D, stage 4->2)
  very loose  n=2  T2D->HF (reverse HF->T2D, stage 4->2); T2D->Stroke (reverse Stroke->T2D, stage 4->2)

What DOES move is the concordant/indeterminate split, which is why the counts are reported as
bound-conditional. Tightening the bound makes an excluded reverse effect harder to declare;
loosening it makes a supported reverse effect harder to declare.
