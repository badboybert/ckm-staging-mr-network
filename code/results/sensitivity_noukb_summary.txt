SCOPE OF THE UKB-FREE NETWORKS: each is the primary edge table with every edge whose OUTCOME is in
{CAD,HF,T2D,Stroke,eGFR} replaced by its UKB-free provider estimate. Four Stroke-as-exposure edges
(Stroke->CAD, Stroke->HF, Stroke->T2D, Stroke->eGFR) have no UKB-free counterpart in the provider
GWAS and retain their PRIMARY values; every other edge into those five outcomes is swapped.

VERDICT:
  - 13 headline FORWARD edges retain SIGN and Bonferroni significance under outcome-side UKB
    removal; 0 attenuate below the bar.
  - 3 headline edges remain NULL in both (SBP->eGFR, BMI->eGFR, HF->T2D) — matching the primary.
  - Staging order stays concordant beyond chance in BOTH UKB-free networks (concordance 0.960, exact P=7.58e-04 [1/1320] MAIN; 0.913, exact P=7.58e-04 [1/1320] FinnGen;
    primary 0.926, exact P=1.01e-03 [2/1980]). Directional architecture is ROBUST to outcome-side UKB overlap.
  - FEEDBACK nuance: CAD->T2D survives in independent FinnGen -> NOT a UKB artifact, but attenuates
    below Bonferroni in Mahajan-noUKB -> partly overlap/power. HF->T2D attenuates in both noUKB
    providers and its Steiger direction flips to FALSE (N_const-sensitive), so it drops from the
    significant DAG -> the HF->T2D backward feedback is the one FRAGILE edge.
  - Net effect on staging: the primary network's backward set [('CAD', 'SBP'), ('CAD', 'T2D')] becomes [('CAD', 'SBP')]
    under MAIN and [('CAD', 'SBP'), ('CAD', 'T2D')] under FinnGen; the AHA-staging conclusion is not weakened by UKB removal.
