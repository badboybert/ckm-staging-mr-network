READ:
- BMI->HF is COVARIANCE-ROBUST: across all 80 combinations of the three swept
  correlations the product-method proportion mediated stays within [15%, 27%].
  'Predominantly direct' does not depend on any covariance assumption.
- T2D->HF is NOT: its interval is materially sensitive to corr(a,total), the correlation the
  first version of this script omitted. a = T2D->CAD and total = T2D->HF are both univariable
  estimates from the SAME T2D instruments, so a positive correlation is expected, and a sits
  in the numerator of (a*b)/total while total sits in the denominator.
    corr(a,total) = 0.0 (as shipped) -> product CI about [38%, 142%]
    corr(a,total) = 0.9              -> product CI about [44%, 116%]
  So the shipped interval is CONSERVATIVE (too wide), not too narrow, and the earlier claim
  that T2D imprecision is 'intrinsic and not an artifact of the independence assumption' was
  WRONG - it was an artifact of omitting this term.
- The defensible statement for the manuscript is therefore NOT that the mediated proportion is
  irreducibly imprecise, but that its interval DEPENDS on a covariance the summary-data design
  cannot identify: under independence it spans complete mediation, under strong shared-
  instrument correlation it does not. Report the range and the dependence, and do not claim
  the mechanism is resolved either way.
- Point estimates are unchanged throughout (they are functions of the estimates alone).
