=== Does the interaction family survive a single global EUR->EAS scale offset? ===

Family: 67 magnitude-eligible edges; Bonferroni 0.05/67 = 7.463e-04
Global IV-weighted slope through the origin:  b_EAS = 0.4360 x b_EUR   (SE 0.0260, z vs 1 = -21.68)
Regression-dilution correction factor: 1.0114  -> dilution-corrected slope 0.4410
  (a correction factor near 1 means the offset is NOT explained by measurement error in b_EUR)

Opposite-sign among survivors: raw 9, absorbed 5

READ:
- The two analyses share only 6 of 14 / 12 edges. The IDENTITY of the
  'significant ancestry interactions' is therefore NOT robust to a single nuisance parameter,
  and no per-edge interaction claim should be made without disclosing this.
- The offset is large (0.44) and precisely estimated (z = -21.7 against 1). It is not
  regression dilution (correction factor 1.011).
- The per-exposure spread above is the diagnostic that matters for the scale dictionary: traits
  it marks equally comparable behave very differently, which is what rank-inverse-normalising
  skewed traits (TG, HDL) against per-SD European traits would produce.
- The manuscript already applies exactly this logic to SBP->CKD ('a power difference, not a
  proven divergence'). Consistency requires extending it to the rest of the family.
