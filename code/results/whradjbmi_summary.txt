FINDING: central fat distribution INDEPENDENTLY drives CAD (+0.29, p=9e-14) and T2D (+0.69,
p=2e-18) -> the adiposity->CAD/T2D effects are not purely about overall mass; distribution matters.
DISSOCIATION: WHRadjBMI does NOT drive heart failure (all-cause HF null p=0.63; niHF -0.13), while
BMI drives HF strongly (+0.52, esp. HFpEF). So the adiposity->HF effect is carried by OVERALL
adiposity/mass, not central distribution -> complements the HF-subtype finding (obesity->HFpEF).

CAVEATS (disclose, load-bearing):
1. COLLIDER: WHRadjBMI is WHR genetically adjusted for BMI; conditioning on a heritable covariate
   can induce collider bias. The NEGATIVE WHRadjBMI->niHF (-0.13) is likely collider-induced, NOT
   causal -> do NOT interpret the inverse HF association as protective; report as null-to-inverse
   with the collider caveat. The positive CAD/T2D effects are robust and directionally standard.
2. UKB OVERLAP: WHRadjBMI (GIANT+UKBB) shares UK Biobank with the CAD (Aragam2022), HF (HERMES) AND
   T2D (Xue2018, GCST006867) outcome GWAS -> exposure-outcome sample overlap biases toward
   observational. This applies to BOTH headline positives (WHRadjBMI->CAD and ->T2D). Direction is
   more robust than magnitude; the UKB-free outcome sensitivity is the clean follow-up if kept
   (UKB-free T2D = data/uploads/eur_noukb/T2D_Mahajan_noUKB_rsid.txt already in hand).
