=== CAC STAGE-3 NODE — Kavousi 2023 (GCST90278456, EUR, GRCh37), added 2026-07-19 ===
Addresses peer-review C2 / the 2026 CKM guideline: the network jumped stage 2 (metabolic) to
stage 4 (clinical CVD) with NO stage-3 subclinical tier. CAC is the guideline-named stage-3 marker.
CAC clumped to 7 independent instruments (thin, as expected). 15 CAC edges in results/network_cac.csv.

STAGE-3 CROSS-STAGE EDGES (all forward, Steiger-correct):
  stage 1/2 -> CAC (robust, hundreds of instruments):
    BMI->CAC +0.49 (p=3.3e-13), LDL->CAC +0.56 (3.1e-16), TC->CAC +0.48 (6.0e-16),
    TG->CAC +0.40 (2.6e-10), HDL->CAC -0.26 (1.4e-5, protective), SBP->CAC +0.021 (3.6e-5)
  stage 3 -> 4 (the subclinical->clinical bridge; THIN, 5-6 instruments):
    CAC->CAD +0.38 (p=2.5e-6), CAC->HF +0.044 (1.2e-2, nominal), CAC->Stroke null (coronary-specific)
  disease->CAC correctly flagged Steiger-REVERSE (CAD/HF/Stroke->CAC).

STAGING TEST WITH THE STAGE-3 TIER (13 nodes, 147 edges, Bonferroni 0.05/147):
  concordance = 0.941 (32/34 forward cross-stage); permutation P = 1.2e-4 (exact, 3/25,740).
  vs 132-edge no-stage-3: 0.926, P=1.5e-3. Adding a genuine stage-3 tier IMPROVES both.

CAVEAT (disclose): CAC->CAD rests on 5 instruments (harmonised file has 6; the 9p21 lead rs4977575
is dropped by harmonise action=2 as an ambiguous G/C palindrome at EAF~0.50). It is pleiotropy-prone
via PHACTR1 (rs9349379) and ADAMTS7 (rs4887109), both shared CAC/CAD loci. The robust contribution of
the CAC node is the stage 1/2 -> CAC direction (hundreds of instruments each). Dropping the thin
CAC->CAD leaves concordance 0.939 (31/33) with all remaining CAC edges forward (the 2 backward edges
are the pre-existing CAD->SBP and CAD->T2D, not CAC edges) -> conclusion robust to the thin edge.

FRAMING (disclose): the 0.926->0.941 gain is partly mechanical — appending 7 all-forward,
Steiger-determined CAC cross-stage edges necessarily lifts a sub-1 ratio. The load-bearing result is
the ABSENCE of any backward CAC edge (no disease->CAC or CAC->lower-stage edge is significant AND
Steiger-forward), i.e. the stage-3 tier inserts cleanly, not the higher number per se.

PERMUTATION P (exact): the label-permutation null has 25,740 distinct stage-label assignments
to the 13 nodes, enumerated exhaustively; 3 reach concordance >= 0.941, so the exact
permutation P is 3/25,740 = 1.2e-4. Node order and random seed do not enter — this is
the exact tail of the null, not a Monte-Carlo estimate of it.
